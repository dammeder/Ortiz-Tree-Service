# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners and property owners in Philadelphia, nearby Pennsylvania counties,
and South Jersey who need tree removal, trimming, stump grinding, lot clearing,
or urgent storm cleanup. Many visitors will arrive on a phone while comparing
local crews or dealing with a time-sensitive hazard.

## Product Purpose

Turn local search and referral traffic into qualified calls and estimate
requests for Ozzy's Tree Service. Success means visitors quickly understand the
services, coverage area, safety credentials, and next action without digging
through repetitive marketing copy.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, and direct. The site should feel like a real working crew:
practical, safety-conscious, responsive, and proud of clean work. It should
build confidence without sounding corporate or overproduced.

## Anti-references

- Generic contractor templates with interchangeable icon cards and filler copy.
- Luxury landscaping sites that make tree work feel precious or delicate.
- Aggressive lead funnels with popups, fake urgency, or repeated calls to action.
- Over-polished stock-photo advertising that hides the actual work.
- Dense pages that restate the same safety, cleanup, and trust claims in several sections.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work early. Realistic job imagery should carry more trust than claims.
2. Make the next action obvious. Calling and requesting an estimate should never require searching.
3. Earn every section. Each block must add new information or reduce a real customer concern.
4. Design mobile first. The primary experience is a homeowner using one hand on a phone.
5. Use proof with restraint. Credentials, service coverage, and reviews should support the decision without repeating themselves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus states, readable contrast,
generous mobile tap targets, descriptive image alternatives, and support for
reduced motion. Forms must provide clear labels, validation feedback, and a
call option for visitors who do not want to submit online.
