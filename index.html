Our Services

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled takedowns of dead, damaged, or hazardous trees. We plan the drop, protect what's around it, and haul everything off.

Tree Trimming

Pruning and shaping for healthier trees and clear rooflines. Clean cuts, no topping, branches kept off the house and power lines.

Stump Grinding

We grind stumps below grade so you can re-seed, plant, or build. Leave the chips or we take them — your call.

Emergency Service

Tree on a structure, hanging limbs, or blocked access? Call Ozzy anytime for urgent help.

Storm Damage

After the immediate danger is handled, we remove fallen limbs and storm debris and clean up the site.

Lot Clearing

Brush and tree clearing to prep for fencing, landscaping, or a build. We clear exactly what you need and grade it clean.
